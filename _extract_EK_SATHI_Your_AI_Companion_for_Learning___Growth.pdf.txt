Problem
Statement
Students today rely on multiple platforms for learning, project
development, career planning, opportunities and technical
updates. These activities are scattered across different tools
and websites, making the overall student journey fragmented
and difficult to manage.
Learning, projects and career activities are handled across
multiple platforms
General AI assistants lack continuous student-specific context
and personalization
GitHub repositories, websites, internships and hackathons
require separate searching and understanding
Useful technology updates are scattered across different
sources
Students need a single platform that connects learning,
building, career and opportunities

Motivation &
Objectives
Students use multiple disconnected
platforms for learning, projects and career
growth.
Useful resources are available, but are not
connected to the student's personal goals.
Existing AI assistants provide general help
but limited student-specific continuity.
We want to build one AI companion that
stays relevant to the student's journey.
The project also gives us an opportunity to
combine AI + Cloud + APIs + Data +
Personalization in one system.
MOTIVATION
OBJECTIVES
Develop a cloud-based AI student companion.
Provide personalized assistance through a
central AI chat.
Support projects and hackathon preparation.
Enable GitHub & Website Study through AI-
based analysis.
Provide resume analysis and personalized
career roadmaps.
Discover relevant internships and
hackathons.
Deliver useful technology news and
personalized updates.
Maintain relevant student memory and
preferences.
